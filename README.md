# learn ai research with phoebe

Two tracks into research a hostile reviewer cannot dismantle, by Phoebe Fu.

**Live:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ai-research-with-phoebe/

AI made reading fast and made being wrong fast too. This course is about the layer that decides
whether you can act on a summary: how good the evidence underneath actually is.

- **Leader track (6 x 45 min)** - for research, insights and strategy leads: which pipeline stages
  really compress, the shared evidence standard, the documented failure modes, the consent and
  confidentiality rails, the stack with its real limits, and deciding on fast evidence.
- **Researcher track (10 x 45 min)** - for analysts, academics, UX and market researchers: one real
  decision run end to end, from question framing through tiered search, screening, extraction,
  contradiction and replication checks, to a memo with an audit trail.

**Scope line:** catching fabricated citations belongs to
[learn ai law with phoebe](https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ai-law-with-phoebe/). This course owns
whether a *real* source actually supports the sentence written on top of it.

## The evidence lab

`assets/research-live.js` runs a real rule engine over a 22-source corpus: ten claims, seven audit
rules, and a verdict per claim traceable to a specific source's tier, sample size, funder or
contradiction. Turn on the five practices and surviving claims climb **0 → 1 → 2 → 4 → 7 → 9 of 10**.
Then press "add 40 sources": your reading pile goes to 62 and a clean claim dies on a retracted paper.

The corpus is a teaching corpus. The audit is not scripted.

## Sources

Taught from PRISMA 2020, Cochrane RoB 2, GRADE, the replication literature (OSC 2015, Camerer 2016 and
2018, Many Labs 1 and 2), the ASA statement on p-values, Braun and Clarke, Landis and Koch, ICMJE, COPE,
Springer Nature, Science and Elsevier AI policies, the NIH and NSF peer-review notices, GDPR Article 89
and Recital 26, Singapore PDPA, the ICC/ESOMAR Code 2025, AAPOR, and live API checks of OpenAlex,
Crossref and Semantic Scholar. Every fact was fetched from a primary source on 2026-08-08 - see
`materials/official-course-map.md`, which also records what could not be verified.
